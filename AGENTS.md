# Strict Aesthetic & Design Rules (Zero AI Slop)

## 🚫 STRICT PROHIBITIONS (NEVER DO THIS)
1. **NO PULSING DOTS / STATUS BADGES**: Under NO circumstances should you EVER add green pulsing dots (`animate-pulse`, `bg-emerald-500`, `rounded-full animate-pulse`, "available for work" dot pills) or generic AI-slop status badges.
2. **NO HARDCODED LOCATION LABELS**: NEVER insert "Semarang" or location labels into the hero, navigation, or general portfolio sections unless explicitly requested.
3. **NO REPETITIVE NAMES**: Do NOT plaster the user's name multiple times across titles, headers, and badges. Keep the main display headlines focused on craft, engineering, and architecture.
4. **NO UNWANTED DECORATIVE CLUTTER**: Keep the design ultra-clean, architectural, and confident. Never add random decorative cutouts, flower graphics, or multi-frame clutter unless explicitly commanded.
5. **NO SLASHES ('/')**: NEVER use forward slashes '/' as decorative or metadata separators (e.g. no '01 / ABOUT', no 'VOL. I / ISSUE', no 'FULL-STACK / ENGINEER'). Use em-dashes '—', bullets '•', periods, or clean whitespace instead.
6. **NO SQUARE BRACKETS ('[' / ']') IN LABELS**: NEVER wrap editorial labels, section headers, badges, or navigation items in square brackets (e.g. no '[ ABOUT ]', no '[ 01 ]', no '[ WORKS ]', no '[ TABLE OF CONTENTS ]'). Use clean, unadorned uppercase typography with generous tracking instead.
7. **NO FAKE COORDINATES / TECHNICAL CLUTTER**: NEVER add fake geographical coordinates (e.g. no '40.7128° N', no 'LAT/LONG', no GMT timezone offsets, no fake coordinate telemetry). Only include authentic, clean content.
8. **STRICT TWO-FONT SYSTEM ONLY**: Use strictly two fonts across the entire website: (1) the primary editorial display serif ('Newsreader') for headlines, cover stories, and monograph titles, and (2) the clean sans-serif ('Geist Sans') for body copy, labels, and UI navigation. NEVER load or introduce a third monospace or mismatched font.
9. **NO PRETENTIOUS / NPD / CRINGE COPY**: Under NO circumstances should you EVER use pompous, narcissistic, pseudo-philosophical copywriting (e.g. no "in pursuit of resilient systems and quiet computational craft", no "where correctness precedes speed", no pretentious essayist tone). Keep all copy grounded, humble, direct, and professional.
10. **NO YEARS IN HERO LABELS**: NEVER put years (e.g. no '2025', no '2026', no '2025 EDITION', no 'VOL. 2025') in the hero masthead, eyebrows, or hero metadata badges.
11. **HERO EDITORIAL STATEMENT (ZERO REDUNDANT TOC)**: The Hero features a clean, confident single-focus editorial statement with generous whitespace, strictly non-cringe professional copy, zero year labels, and direct navigation links to Selected Work, Writings, and Cultural Shelf without redundant project list duplications.
